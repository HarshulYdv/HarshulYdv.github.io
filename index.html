Railway Predictive Maintenance
Sensor-based failure prediction, anomaly detection, maintenance prioritization and fastener defect classification. XGBoost, Isolation Forest and EfficientNetB0 sit behind two FastAPI services and a browser dashboard for fleet monitoring and image inspection. 85% overall accuracy on binary failure prediction and about 91.4% validation accuracy on fastener images.

2026
Illegal Logging Detection
A ResNet18 model that sorts rainforest audio into seven categories: 81.2% test accuracy, 83.3% precision and 0.937 AUC. Live microphone monitoring uses five-second windows with three-sample smoothing and cut false positives by 60%. 128-band Mel spectrograms, four augmentations (147 samples grown to 735) and a Gradio interface with live waveform and detection history.
